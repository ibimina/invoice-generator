## Template Structure

All templates follow a consistent structure:

1. **Header** - Business info and document title
2. **Client Section** - Bill to information
3. **Details Section** - Document number, dates
4. **Items Table** - Line items with quantities and prices
5. **Totals Section** - Subtotal, tax, discounts, grand total
6. **Footer** - Notes and terms

## Customization

### Changing Colors
Select any of the 10 preset accent colors during template selection.

### Adding a Logo
Upload your company logo in the business details step.

### Custom Terms
Add your own terms and conditions in the document details.

## PDF vs Preview

Templates render identically in:
- **Live Preview** - HTML/CSS rendering for real-time feedback
- **PDF Export** - React-PDF rendering for downloadable documents

Minor differences may occur due to PDF rendering constraints.
